01 / ABOUT

Engineer. Coordinator. Problem solver.

I am a Civil Engineer with an MSc in Construction Project Management from UWE Bristol and professional experience spanning site engineering, project execution, procurement and construction coordination.

My industry experience includes work on a major economic-zone infrastructure project, hospital construction, residential building construction and commercial building work. I enjoy connecting technical site requirements with planning, procurement, people and data.

My academic interests are moving toward digital construction and circularity, particularly the use of BIM, digital material passports, blockchain/DLT and sustainable procurement to improve material traceability, reuse and construction-waste reduction.
